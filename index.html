About Sri Lankan Wildlife

Sri Lanka is a biodiversity hotspot with a wide range of ecosystems, from rainforests and dry forests to wetlands and coastal habitats. The island is home to elephants, leopards, endemic birds, reptiles, amphibians, and countless plant species. Many of these species are unique to Sri Lanka, making it a paradise for nature lovers and conservationists.
